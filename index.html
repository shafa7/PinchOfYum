These Chicken Tacos In A Nutshell

1

SEASON THE CHICKEN.


Three primary ingredients: taco seasoning, garlic, and a shot of soy sauce! Im showing the taco seasoning on the chicken first in this picture, but Ive come to realize that mixing works best if you start with the wet stuff (oil and soy sauce) and then finish with a quick toss in the dry spices! Doesnt get quite so clumpy.

2

COOK THE CHICKEN.


I like to air fry it : 400 degrees for 11 minutes. Alternate methods below if you dont have an air fryer!

3

MAKE A GREEN SAUCE.


Not essential, but this is the kind of sauce that makes life worth living.

4

CUT UP THE CHICKEN.


Just give it a good rough chop into little bite-sized pieces, like how it is at Chipotle. While you have the cutting board out, go ahead and chop some cilantro and onions, too!

5

YOURE DONE! YUM!


Holy smokes. I can eat a lot of these
